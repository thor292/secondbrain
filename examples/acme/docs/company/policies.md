# Acme Retail company policies

Annual leave: every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per year. Unused leave of up to 5 days can be carried over to the next year.

Travel reimbursement: the travel reimbursement limit is 500 dollars per trip. Claims must be submitted within 30 days with receipts.

Working hours: core working hours are 10am to 4pm. Remote work is allowed two days per week with manager approval.
