# Desi Living Employee Handbook

## Earned leave
Every full-time employee gets 18 days of paid earned leave per financial year (April to March), credited monthly at 1.5 days. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next year; carried-forward leave above 30 days lapses. Unused earned leave is paid out when you leave Desi Living. Part-time employees get leave pro rata.

## Casual and sick leave
Employees get 7 days of casual leave and 10 days of paid sick leave per financial year. Casual and sick leave cannot be carried forward. A doctor's certificate is needed for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Festive peak and leave
From the festive sale start until 2 days after Diwali, Operations and Customer Support staff cannot take planned leave, except for emergencies approved by their department head. Warehouse staff who work Sundays in this period get a compensatory day off for each Sunday, to be used by 31 January.

## Parental leave
Women employees get 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave, as the Maternity Benefit Act requires, and 12 weeks when adopting a child under 3 months. Fathers and secondary caregivers get 4 weeks of fully paid paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## Remote work
Office teams work from the Indiranagar office in Bengaluru on Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays. Mondays and Fridays are remote by default. Warehouse and fulfillment roles in Hoskote and Bhiwandi are on site.

## Benefits
Every employee is covered by group health insurance of ₹5 lakh per year for the employee, spouse, children and parents. Employer provident fund (PF) is 12 percent of basic pay. Gratuity is paid after 5 years of service as the law requires.

## Equipment allowance
New employees get a one-time home office allowance of ₹25,000, and a laptop is provided by IT. Replacement equipment is requested through the IT help desk in Slack (#it-help).

## Expenses and travel
Business travel needs manager approval in advance. Flights are economy class; train travel is 2AC. Meals and local travel are covered up to ₹1,500 per day in metro cities and ₹1,000 elsewhere.
| Level | Hotel cap per night |
| Employee | ₹4,500 |
| Manager | ₹6,500 |
| Department head and above | ₹9,000 |
Expense claims are submitted in Zoho Expense within 30 days with GST bills where available.

## Code of conduct
We treat customers, artisans, suppliers and each other with respect. Gifts from suppliers above ₹2,000 in value, including Diwali gift boxes, must be declined or reported to People & HR. Customer personal data, phone numbers and addresses are never shared outside the support and fulfillment tools. Complaints of harassment go to the Internal Committee under the POSH Act; Farah Khan, People partner, is the committee's presiding contact.
