# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
Customers can return unused items in original packaging within 10 days of delivery for a full refund. Festive orders placed between 3 October and 8 November 2026 (the Festive Sale through Diwali) can be returned until 30 November 2026.

## Free returns
Return pickup is free for orders of ₹999 or more; Delhivery collects the item from the customer's address. For smaller orders a flat ₹99 return pickup fee is deducted from the refund. Returns are not picked up from Zone C pincodes; those customers send the item by India Post and we refund the postage up to ₹150.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued after the warehouse checks the returned item, within 2 business days of receiving it. Prepaid orders (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets) are refunded to the original payment method in 5 to 7 business days. COD orders are refunded to the customer's UPI ID or bank account within 7 business days. Store credit is issued instantly and includes a 10 percent bonus.

## Damaged or defective items
Items that arrive damaged must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with a photo or unboxing video, on WhatsApp or by email. They are replaced free of charge and the replacement ships within 3 business days. Customers are not asked to return damaged items under ₹3,000; for items above ₹3,000, such as brass floor lamps and furniture, we collect the damaged piece when we deliver the replacement.

## Furniture
Sofas and side tables can be returned within 10 days only if they are damaged, defective or not as described. BharatFleet collects them; the customer pays nothing. Change-of-mind returns of furniture are not accepted once the piece has been unpacked and placed.

## Final sale
Clearance items marked final sale, personalised or monogrammed pieces, and opened diya & candle sets cannot be returned.

## Marketplace and store purchases
Orders placed on JioMart follow JioMart's return policy and are handled through JioMart. Purchases from a DMart shop-in-shop can be returned at the same DMart store within 7 days with the bill.
