# Q2 FY27 Financial Results (July to September 2026)

## Headline numbers
Net revenue for Q2 FY27 (July to September 2026) was ₹11.9 crore, up 21 percent year over year from ₹9.9 crore in Q2 FY26. Gross margin was 47 percent, down from 49 percent last year because of higher freight costs in the monsoon and BharatFleet's 6 percent rate increase in April. EBITDA was ₹71 lakh, an EBITDA margin of 6 percent.

## Revenue by category
Textiles were 42 percent of revenue, lighting 27 percent, furniture 26 percent and decor 5 percent. The Independence Day sale (13 to 16 August) brought ₹1.1 crore in four days. Raksha Bandhan gifting lifted kantha throw and diya set sales in the last two weeks of August.

## Returns
The return rate was 8.4 percent of revenue. Damage-in-transit returns of brass floor lamps rose in September because of weak cartons from Noor Metalcraft; Merchandising has asked for stronger packaging.

## First half
Revenue for the first half of FY27 (April to September 2026) was ₹22.4 crore: ₹10.5 crore in Q1 and ₹11.9 crore in Q2.

## Cash
Cash at the end of September was ₹14.6 crore. Operations were cash positive in the quarter; we spent ₹3.2 crore building festive inventory ahead of Diwali.

## Outlook
Full year FY2026-27 revenue is forecast at ₹58 crore, up 20 percent on FY2025-26 (₹48.3 crore). Q3 FY27 (October to December), which includes Diwali and the start of the wedding season, is forecast at ₹19.5 crore. The board asked management to protect gross margin above 45 percent through the festive season.
