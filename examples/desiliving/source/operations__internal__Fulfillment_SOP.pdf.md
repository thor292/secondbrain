# Fulfillment Standard Operating Procedure

## Warehouses
Hoskote, Bengaluru is our main warehouse and ships all South and East India orders and all furniture. Bhiwandi, Mumbai ships West and North India parcel orders and replenishes the 40 DMart shop-in-shops in Mumbai and Pune every Tuesday and Friday. Vikram Rao, Operations manager, runs both sites; Priya Nair, Warehouse coordinator, runs the daily plan and inbound quality checks.

## Order cutoff
Parcel orders placed before 1pm IST on business days ship the same day. Orders after the cutoff ship the next business day. JioMart orders must be handed to the carrier within 24 hours of the order, as JioMart's seller SLA requires.

## Pick, pack and ship targets
Standard orders are picked and packed within 4 hours. Express orders are prioritised and leave on the 6pm Blue Dart pickup. Delhivery collects at 4pm and 8pm. Furniture is booked with BharatFleet the same day and leaves Hoskote within 2 business days.

## Packing fragile pieces
Brass lamps, diya sets and Khurja ceramics are double boxed with honeycomb paper and corner protectors; we do not use plastic bubble wrap. Brass floor lamps are packed with the base and the stem in separate inner boxes. Dhurries and textiles are wrapped in a waterproof sleeve from June to September for the monsoon. Furniture is shrink-wrapped and edge-protected.

## Carriers
Delhivery handles standard parcels in Zone A and Zone B. Blue Dart handles express parcels. India Post Speed Post handles Zone C pincodes. BharatFleet Logistics handles sofas and side tables with 4 to 10 business day delivery windows, including unpacking and placement.

## Damaged inbound stock
Damaged inbound units are photographed, quarantined in bay Q at Hoskote or bay 12 at Bhiwandi, and reported to Merchandising (Kabir Singh) within 24 hours so the supplier can be charged back with a debit note. If more than 2 percent of a batch is damaged, the whole batch is checked before any unit is sold. Repeated damage from the same supplier is raised in the #ops-alerts Slack channel.

## Customer damage reports
When Support reports a damaged item from a customer, the warehouse coordinator checks the remaining stock from the same batch within 1 business day and tells Support and Merchandising what was found.

## Peak season
From the festive sale start until Diwali, and again for the first two weeks of the wedding season, both warehouses run two shifts and work Sundays. Temporary staff complete a 1 day safety induction before working on the floor. The Diwali dispatch plan is fixed by 15 September each year.
