# Diwali Campaign Plan 2026

## Overview
The festive season, from the Festive Sale start to Diwali, is about 21 percent of our annual revenue. In 2026 Dhanteras is Friday 6 November and Diwali is Sunday 8 November. The theme this year is "Ghar ki roshni" (light for your home), featuring Moradabad brass lighting, diya sets, kantha throws and gifts under ₹2,000. Rohan Kapoor, Marketing lead, owns the plan; Sana Sheikh owns content and email.

## Offers
Festive Sale: up to 20 percent off sitewide, excluding new arrivals and final sale items; the sofa is limited to 15 percent off. Brass diya & sandalwood candle set at ₹799 from 1 November. Free gift wrap and a handwritten card on all orders from 20 October to 6 November. Ghar Club members earn double points from 1 to 8 November. Corporate gifting: 10 percent off orders of 50 or more diya sets.

## Channels
WhatsApp broadcasts and email to the 3.2 lakh subscriber list, paid social on Instagram and Facebook, YouTube Shorts, Google search, JioMart sponsored listings, and creator partnerships with home decor creators posting in Hindi and English.

## Success metrics
Target festive revenue of ₹9.5 crore from 3 October to 8 November across all channels, a desiliving.in conversion rate of 2.8 percent, a WhatsApp revenue share of 22 percent, and ₹60 lakh of corporate gifting orders.
