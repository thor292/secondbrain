# Pricing and Discount Policy

## MRP rules
Every piece has one MRP (maximum retail price) printed on the pack, inclusive of GST, and the same MRP is used on desiliving.in, JioMart and in DMart. We never sell above MRP. MRP changes are approved by the CFO and take effect on the 1st of a month, with new packs printed before the change.

## Discount limits
Festive and sale discounts are at most 20 percent off MRP, except clearance which can go to 50 percent. New arrivals cannot be discounted in their first 30 days after launch. Coupon codes cannot be stacked on top of a sale price.

## Margin floor
Every promotion must keep the product's gross margin at or above 35 percent after the discount. Our landed cost is about 52 percent of MRP on average, so a 20 percent discount is the deepest a typical piece can go and still clear the floor. Merchandising checks this before a promotion goes live; clearance below the floor needs the CFO's approval.

## Marketplaces
JioMart sale prices must match desiliving.in sale prices on the same day. JioMart's commission is about 18 percent, so pieces near the margin floor are not put in JioMart's own extra-discount events. DMart prices are the MRP minus any discount agreed with DMart in writing.
