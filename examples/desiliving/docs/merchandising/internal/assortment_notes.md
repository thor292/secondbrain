# Assortment Notes Q3 FY27 (October to December 2026)

## Bestsellers
Top sellers this festive season are the Moradabad brass floor lamp, the Kantha cotton throw, the Brass diya & sandalwood candle set and the Jaipur block-print bedsheet set. The diya set is our top Diwali gift and sells about three times its normal rate in Dhanteras week.

## Stock risks
The Sheesham & linen 3-seater sofa is on backorder; Saharanpur Woodcraft's lead time is 7 weeks. The Moradabad brass floor lamp has about 3 weeks of stock at the current sales rate and runs out around the end of November unless we reorder now. The diya & candle set is expected to sell out around Diwali. The Sheesham wood side table has more than 6 months of stock and is a candidate for the Republic Day sale.

## Damaged supplier stock
Kabir Singh (Merchandiser) owns supplier quality and damage claims. When the warehouse reports damaged inbound units, Kabir raises a debit note with the supplier within 2 business days. Noor Metalcraft brass lamps have had repeated transit damage to the lamp bases; Kabir has asked Noor Metalcraft for corner protectors and double-wall cartons.

## Channel assortment
JioMart carries all eight pieces except the sofa. The DMart shop-in-shops carry the kantha throw, diya set, block-print bedsheet and ceramic lamp only, because they fit the shelf and sell as gifts.
