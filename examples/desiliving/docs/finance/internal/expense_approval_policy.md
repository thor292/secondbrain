# Spending and Approval Policy

## Approval limits
Managers can approve purchases up to ₹2 lakh. Department heads can approve up to ₹10 lakh. Anything above ₹10 lakh needs the CFO's approval, and above ₹50 lakh also the CEO's.

## Invoices
Every supplier invoice must be a GST invoice showing the supplier's GSTIN and must reference a purchase order in Zoho Books. Suppliers are paid on net 45 day terms. Artisan workshops registered as MSMEs, such as Noor Metalcraft and Panipat Looms, must be paid within 45 days by law, so Finance pays them first in every payment run. TDS is deducted where it applies.

## Payment runs
Supplier payments go out every Tuesday and Friday. Before Diwali Finance runs an extra payment on the Monday of Dhanteras week so artisans are paid before the festival.

## Month-end close
The books are closed on the 5th business day of each month. Department heads receive their budget versus actual report on the 7th business day. GST returns (GSTR-1) are filed by the 11th of each month.
