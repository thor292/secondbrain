# Brand Voice Guide

## How we sound
Warm, rooted and simple. We write like a friend who knows the craft, not like a catalog. Short sentences, plain English, and never more than one exclamation mark per message. We may use a Hindi word where it is natural, such as ghar, diya or utsav, but we do not write in slang.

## Words we use
Handmade, crafted, ghar, everyday, artisan, heirloom. We name the craft and the place: "Moradabad brass", "Sanganer block print", "Panipat dhurrie". We say "pieces" instead of "products" and "your home" instead of "your property".

## Words we avoid
Cheap, luxury, ethnic, exotic, "dhamaka", "lowest price ever", "limited stock" unless it is true, and any sustainability or "eco" claims that legal has not approved.

## WhatsApp and support messages
Customer messages are in English or Hindi, matching the language the customer used. Greet by first name, keep it to three short lines, and always end with what happens next and when.

## Festivals
We wish customers for every major festival, not only Diwali, and we never mix festival greetings with discount codes in the same first line.
