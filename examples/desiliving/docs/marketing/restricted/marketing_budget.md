# Marketing Budget and Performance (Confidential)

## Festive budget
The Diwali 2026 paid media budget is ₹1.6 crore, spent from 20 September to 8 November: 50 percent Meta (Instagram and Facebook), 25 percent Google search and Performance Max, 15 percent creator partnerships, and 10 percent JioMart sponsored listings.

## Full year budget
The FY2026-27 marketing budget is ₹4.6 crore, about 9 percent of planned revenue. Rohan Kapoor owns the budget; spend above plan needs the CFO's approval.

## Acquisition costs
Blended customer acquisition cost (CAC) in Q2 FY27 was ₹640, up from ₹560 a year earlier because of higher Meta costs during the festive build-up. The return on ad spend (ROAS) target for the Diwali season is 4.0. WhatsApp broadcasts cost about ₹0.80 per message and returned ₹22 per rupee spent last Diwali.
