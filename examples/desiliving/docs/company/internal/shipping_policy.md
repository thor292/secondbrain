# Shipping Policy

## Free shipping
Standard shipping is free on orders of ₹999 or more anywhere in India. Below ₹999 standard shipping costs ₹79. These rules apply to orders on desiliving.in; JioMart orders follow JioMart's own shipping rules.

## Pincode zones and delivery times
We group pincodes into three zones. Delivery times are counted from dispatch, in business days.
| Zone | Covers | Standard delivery | Carrier |
| Zone A | Metro cities: Bengaluru, Mumbai, Pune, Delhi NCR, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Ahmedabad | 2 to 4 days | Delhivery |
| Zone B | Other serviceable pincodes, tier 2 and tier 3 cities | 4 to 6 days | Delhivery |
| Zone C | Remote pincodes: North East, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep, hill districts | 7 to 12 days | India Post Speed Post |

## Express shipping
Express shipping costs ₹149 and is delivered by Blue Dart in 1 to 2 business days to Zone A pincodes. Express is not available in Zone C.

## Furniture
Sofas and side tables are delivered by BharatFleet Logistics in 4 to 10 business days depending on the zone, including doorstep delivery, unpacking and placement in the room of choice. Furniture delivery is free. BharatFleet calls the customer a day ahead to fix a delivery slot. Furniture is not delivered to Zone C pincodes.

## Cash on delivery (COD)
COD is available on orders up to ₹10,000 in Zone A and Zone B, with a COD fee of ₹49. COD is not available for furniture, for orders above ₹10,000, or for Zone C pincodes. Customers who refuse two COD orders lose COD for 6 months.

## Diwali shipping cutoff
Orders placed by 2 days before Dhanteras arrive before Diwali with standard shipping. For Diwali 2026 the standard shipping cutoff is Wednesday 4 November 2026. Blue Dart express orders to Zone A pincodes placed by 12 noon on Dhanteras, Friday 6 November, arrive before Diwali on Sunday 8 November. The cutoff for furniture to arrive before Diwali was 22 October. Orders after these dates ship normally but are not guaranteed before Diwali.

## International
We ship within India only.
