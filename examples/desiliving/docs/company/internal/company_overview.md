# Desi Living Company Overview

## Who we are
Desi Living is a direct-to-consumer home decor and lifestyle brand founded in 2019 and based in Indiranagar, Bengaluru. We sell handmade Indian home goods: Moradabad brass lighting, Jaipur block-print textiles, Panipat dhurries, Saharanpur sheesham wood furniture and Khurja ceramics. We have about 140 employees, 60 of them in our two warehouses. Meera Iyer is our founder and CEO and Anand Krishnan is our CFO.

## Where we sell
Our own Shopify store, desiliving.in, brings about 55 percent of revenue. The Reliance JioMart marketplace brings about 30 percent. Shop-in-shop shelves in 40 DMart stores in Mumbai and Pune bring about 15 percent.

## Warehouses and carriers
Our main warehouse is in Hoskote, Bengaluru. Our second warehouse is in Bhiwandi, Mumbai, and also replenishes the DMart stores. BharatFleet Logistics delivers large items such as sofas and tables. Delhivery and Blue Dart carry parcels, and India Post (Speed Post) serves remote pincodes.

## Suppliers
Noor Metalcraft in Moradabad makes our brass lamps and diya sets. Sanganer Prints in Jaipur prints our bedsheets. Panipat Looms weaves our dhurries and kantha throws. Saharanpur Woodcraft makes our sheesham furniture. Khurja Ceramics Co. makes our ceramic lamps.

## Mission and values
Our mission is to bring India's craft traditions into everyday homes at fair prices, and to pay the artisans who make them fairly and on time. Our values are: customers first, respect the maker, own the outcome, and keep it simple.

## Revenue and goals for FY2026-27
Annual revenue is about 48 crore rupees and growing about 20 percent a year. FY2025-26 revenue was ₹48.3 crore. Goals for FY2026-27 (April 2026 to March 2027): reach ₹58 crore in revenue, keep the return rate under 8 percent, lift the repeat customer rate to 35 percent, reach a customer satisfaction score (CSAT) of 92 percent, and grow from 40 to 60 DMart stores by March 2027.

## Our calendar
Dhanteras and Diwali are the peak of the year. In 2026 Dhanteras is Friday 6 November and Diwali is Sunday 8 November. The festive sale season starts in late September or early October. Other moments that move sales: the Independence Day sale (15 August), the Republic Day sale (26 January), Raksha Bandhan, Holi, and the wedding seasons (mid-November to mid-December and mid-January to February).
