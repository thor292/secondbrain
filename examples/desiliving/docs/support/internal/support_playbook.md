# Customer Support Playbook

## Channels and languages
WhatsApp Business is our main support channel and handles about 70 percent of tickets. Email and Instagram messages make up the rest; all of them land in Freshdesk. We answer in English or Hindi, matching the customer. Kannada and Marathi speakers on the team take those customers when asked. Phone support is only offered to VIP customers.

## Response time targets
WhatsApp messages are answered within 15 minutes during business hours (9am to 9pm IST, Monday to Saturday; also Sundays from the festive sale start until Diwali). First response on email within 6 business hours.

## VIP customers
Customers with lifetime spend over ₹50,000 are VIP (Ghar Club Gold). Their tickets are tagged automatically in Freshdesk and go to the front of the queue. Always record a VIP's preferred contact channel on their profile and use it.

## Goodwill credits
Support agents can give a goodwill credit of up to ₹500 without approval. Team leads can approve up to ₹2,000. Anything above ₹2,000 needs approval from the Head of Support, Arjun Mehta.

## Escalation
Tier 1 agents handle order status, returns, COD questions and product questions. Tier 2 handles damaged furniture, damaged brass lamps and ceramics, BharatFleet delivery failures, and refund or payment disputes. Legal threats, safety issues and press or social media complaints go straight to the Head of Support.

## Damaged brass lamps
A customer reporting a damaged brass lamp sends a photo or unboxing video on WhatsApp. Tier 2 sends a replacement within 3 business days without asking for the damaged lamp back, tags the ticket "transit-damage" and records the order and batch. The warehouse coordinator (Priya Nair) checks the batch at the warehouse and Merchandising (Kabir Singh) claims the cost back from Noor Metalcraft.

## Where is my order (WISMO)
Check the tracking in Shopify first, or in Unicommerce for JioMart orders. If a standard order has not moved for 2 business days, raise a ticket with Delhivery and offer the customer free express shipping on their next order. Furniture delivery questions go to BharatFleet's customer desk through the Operations team.

## COD orders
When a COD delivery fails, message the customer on WhatsApp within 24 hours to confirm address and slot before the parcel is returned. COD refunds are paid to the customer's UPI ID or bank account, never in cash.

## Marketplace and store customers
JioMart customers are answered through the JioMart seller portal within 24 hours. DMart shop-in-shop customers are sent back to the store for returns within 7 days with their bill.
