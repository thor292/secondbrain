# Carrier Contracts Summary (Confidential)

## BharatFleet Logistics (large items)
BharatFleet Logistics, based in Pune, delivers all furniture: the sheesham sofa and the sheesham side table. The contract runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2028; rates are reviewed every March and went up 6 percent in April 2026. Our contact at BharatFleet is Karan Malhotra, Operations director.

## BharatFleet rates
Rates are per kg of packed weight, from our Hoskote or Bhiwandi warehouse, with a minimum charge of ₹1,200 per consignment. Unpacking, placement and assembly cost ₹450 per item.
| Zone | Covers | Rate per kg | Delivery SLA |
| Zone 1 | Same state or within 300 km of the warehouse | ₹16 | 4 business days |
| Zone 2 | Same region (South India from Hoskote, West India from Bhiwandi) | ₹24 | 7 business days |
| Zone 3 | Rest of India, metros and tier 2 cities | ₹32 | 10 business days |
A festive surcharge of ₹3 per kg applies from 1 October to 15 November.

## What a sofa costs to deliver
The Sheesham & linen 3-seater sofa weighs 68 kg packed. Delivered in Bengaluru from Hoskote (Zone 1) it costs ₹1,200 (minimum charge) plus ₹450 placement, ₹1,650 in all. To Hyderabad or Chennai (Zone 2) it costs ₹1,632 plus ₹450, ₹2,082. To Delhi NCR (Zone 3) it costs ₹2,176 plus ₹450, ₹2,626. In the festive window add ₹204. The side table weighs 14 kg packed and always pays the ₹1,200 minimum plus ₹450.

## BharatFleet service levels and penalties
BharatFleet must deliver 95 percent of consignments within the SLA each month. Late deliveries earn a credit of 5 percent of the freight charge per day late, capped at 25 percent. A missed delivery slot (no-show) earns a ₹500 credit each time. Damage noted on the proof of delivery is paid at invoice value up to ₹50,000 per item; claims must be raised within 7 days of delivery.

## Delhivery (parcels)
Delhivery carries standard parcels up to 10 kg. The contract runs until 31 March 2027 with a volume commitment of 25,000 shipments a month. Rates per 500 g: ₹38 within Zone A, ₹46 to Zone B. The COD fee is 1.75 percent of order value, minimum ₹30. Returned COD parcels (RTO) are charged at the forward rate.

## Blue Dart (express)
Blue Dart carries express parcels at ₹92 per 500 g, next-day delivery to 22 metro cities. A festive surcharge of ₹12 per shipment applies from 15 October to 15 November.

## India Post
Speed Post serves Zone C pincodes that Delhivery does not reach, at ₹55 to ₹120 per parcel by weight. No COD.
