# Compensation Bands FY2026-27 (Confidential)

All figures are fixed annual pay (CTC) in lakh rupees per annum (LPA), including employer PF.

## Customer Support
Support agent: ₹3.6 to 4.8 LPA. Support team lead: ₹6 to 8 LPA. Head of Support: ₹28 to 36 LPA.

## Operations
Warehouse associate: ₹2.6 to 3.2 LPA. Warehouse coordinator: ₹4.5 to 6 LPA. Operations manager: ₹18 to 24 LPA.

## Marketing and Merchandising
Content marketer: ₹7 to 10 LPA. Performance marketer: ₹10 to 14 LPA. Merchandiser: ₹8 to 12 LPA. Marketing lead: ₹22 to 30 LPA.

## Finance
Finance analyst: ₹9 to 13 LPA.

## Annual review and bonus
Salary reviews happen every April at the start of the financial year. The FY2026-27 merit budget is 9 percent of payroll. The statutory bonus for eligible employees is paid with the October salary, before Diwali.
