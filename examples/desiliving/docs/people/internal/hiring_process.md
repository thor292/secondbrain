# Hiring Process

## Stages
Every role goes through a 30 minute recruiter screen, a hiring manager interview, a practical exercise and a final values interview with a department head. We aim to give every candidate a decision within 3 weeks. Support roles include a written test in English and Hindi.

## Referrals
Employees who refer a candidate who is hired and stays 90 days get a referral bonus of ₹40,000, or ₹15,000 for warehouse roles.

## Offers
Offers within the salary band are approved by the department head. Offers above the band need approval from the People partner (Farah Khan) and the CFO. We can buy out up to 30 days of a candidate's notice period with the CFO's approval.

## Background checks
All offers are subject to employment and address verification. Warehouse and delivery roles also need police verification before the first shift.

## Hiring freeze windows
Operations and Support do not start new hires between 15 October and Diwali, so that new people are not trained during the peak.
