# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

## Before day one
IT ships the laptop, People & HR sends the welcome email and the joining documents in Keka (PAN, Aadhaar, bank details for payroll and the PF nomination), and the manager assigns an onboarding buddy.

## First week
Complete the POSH (prevention of sexual harassment) and data privacy training, meet the buddy every day, shadow two WhatsApp support sessions, and read the Employee Handbook. Bengaluru hires spend half a day at the Hoskote warehouse; Mumbai hires visit Bhiwandi and one DMart shop-in-shop.

## First 30 days
Agree on 30, 60 and 90 day goals with the manager. The PF account (UAN) is linked in the first payroll. New hires get a check-in with the People partner at day 30.
