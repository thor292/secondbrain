# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
Customers can return unused items in original packaging within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Holiday orders placed between November 15 and December 24 can be returned until January 31.

## Free returns
Returns are free for orders over 100 dollars. For smaller orders a flat 8 dollar return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the warehouse receives the return. Store credit is issued instantly and includes a 10 percent bonus.

## Damaged or defective items
Items that arrive damaged are replaced free of charge, shipped within 48 hours. The customer is not asked to return damaged items under 150 dollars; a photo is enough.

## Final sale
Clearance items marked final sale, custom upholstery and mattresses that have been unsealed cannot be returned.

## International orders
Orders to Canada and the United Kingdom can be returned within 30 days, but return shipping is paid by the customer.
