# Fulfillment Standard Operating Procedure

## Warehouse
All orders ship from the Reno, Nevada fulfillment center. Large furniture ships from the same site through a white glove delivery partner, Hearth Freight.

## Order cutoff
Orders placed before 2pm Pacific time on business days ship the same day. Orders after the cutoff ship the next business day.

## Pick, pack and ship targets
Standard orders are picked and packed within 4 hours. Express orders are prioritized and leave on the 5pm UPS pickup. Fragile items such as lamps and ceramics are double boxed.

## Carriers
UPS handles standard ground and 2 day express. FedEx is the backup carrier when UPS volume caps are reached. Hearth Freight handles sofas, beds and tables with 2 to 3 week delivery windows.

## Damaged inbound stock
Damaged inbound units are photographed, quarantined in aisle Q and reported to Merchandising within 24 hours so the supplier can be charged back.

## Peak season
From mid November to late December the warehouse runs extended hours. Temporary staff complete a 1 day safety induction before working on the floor.
