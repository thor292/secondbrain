# Lumen Home Employee Handbook

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee gets 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year and must be used by March 31. Part-time employees get leave pro rata.

## Sick leave
Employees get 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is needed for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers get 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave; secondary caregivers get 6 weeks. Leave can start up to 4 weeks before the expected birth or adoption date.

## Remote work
Teams work from the office in Portland on Tuesdays and Thursdays. The other three days are remote by default. Warehouse and fulfillment roles in Reno are on site.

## Equipment allowance
New employees get a one-time home office allowance of 600 dollars, and a laptop is provided by IT. Replacement equipment is requested through the IT help desk in Slack (#it-help).

## Expenses and travel
Business travel needs manager approval in advance. Meals on business travel are covered up to 45 dollars per day. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours.

| Level | Hotel cap per night |
| Employee | 160 dollars |
| Manager | 190 dollars |
| Director and above | 240 dollars |

Expense claims are submitted in Ramp within 30 days with receipts.

## Code of conduct
We treat customers, suppliers and each other with respect. Gifts from suppliers above 75 dollars must be declined or reported to People & HR. Customer personal data is never shared outside the support and fulfillment tools.
