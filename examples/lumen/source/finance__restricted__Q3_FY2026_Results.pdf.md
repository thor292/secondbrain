# Q3 FY2026 Financial Results

## Headline numbers
Net revenue for Q3 was 14.2 million dollars, up 18 percent year over year. Gross margin was 46 percent, down from 48 percent last year because of higher freight costs. EBITDA margin was 6 percent.

## Returns
The return rate was 9.5 percent of revenue. Returns of lighting products fell after the packaging change in August.

## Cash
Cash at the end of the quarter was 9.8 million dollars, enough for more than 24 months at the current burn rate.

## Outlook
Full year revenue is forecast at 61 million dollars. The board asked management to protect gross margin above 45 percent during the holiday season.
