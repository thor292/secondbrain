# Holiday Campaign Plan 2026

## Overview
The holiday season (Black Friday through Christmas) is about 34 percent of annual revenue. The theme this year is "Light up your home", featuring lighting, textiles and gifts under 100 dollars.

## Key dates
Loyalty members get early access to Black Friday prices on November 24. Black Friday sitewide sale runs November 27 to December 1. Gift guide emails go out every Monday in December. Last day for guaranteed Christmas delivery with standard shipping is December 17.

## Offers
Sitewide 20 percent off during Black Friday weekend, excluding new arrivals and final sale items. Free gift wrap on all orders in December. Loyalty members earn double points from November 24 to December 1.

## Channels
Email and SMS to the 410,000 subscriber list, paid social on Instagram and Pinterest, and creator partnerships with home decor influencers.

## Success metrics
Target holiday revenue of 5.6 million dollars, conversion rate of 3.2 percent, and email revenue share of 28 percent.
