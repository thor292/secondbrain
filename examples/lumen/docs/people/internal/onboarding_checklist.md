# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

## Before day one
IT ships the laptop, People & HR sends the welcome email and the manager assigns an onboarding buddy.

## First week
Complete security and privacy training, meet the buddy every day, shadow two customer support sessions, and read the Employee Handbook.

## First 30 days
Agree on 30, 60 and 90 day goals with the manager. New hires get a check-in with the People partner at day 30.
