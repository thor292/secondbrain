# Hiring Process

## Stages
Every role goes through a 30 minute recruiter screen, a hiring manager interview, a practical exercise and a final values interview. We aim to give every candidate a decision within 3 weeks.

## Referrals
Employees who refer a candidate who is hired and stays 90 days get a referral bonus of 1,500 dollars.

## Offers
Offers within the salary band are approved by the department head. Offers above the band need approval from the People partner and the CFO.
