# Pricing and Discount Policy

## Discount limits
Promotions can discount at most 30 percent, except clearance which can go to 60 percent. New arrivals cannot be discounted in their first 30 days after launch.

## Margin floor
Every promotion must keep the product's gross margin above 35 percent after the discount. Merchandising checks this before a promotion goes live.

## Partner brands
Partner brands such as Nordlys lighting have a minimum advertised price (MAP). Their products can never be shown below MAP on the site or in ads.
