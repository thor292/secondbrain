# Customer Support Playbook

## Response time targets
First response on email within 4 business hours. Live chat answered within 2 minutes during business hours (7am to 7pm Pacific, Monday to Saturday). Phone support is only offered to VIP customers.

## VIP customers
Customers with lifetime spend over 2,000 dollars are VIP. Their tickets are tagged automatically in Zendesk and go to the front of the queue.

## Goodwill credits
Support agents can give a goodwill credit of up to 25 dollars without approval. Team leads can approve up to 100 dollars. Anything above 100 dollars needs approval from the Head of Support.

## Escalation
Tier 1 agents handle order status, returns and product questions. Tier 2 handles damaged furniture, delivery failures and payment disputes. Legal threats, safety issues and press inquiries go straight to the Head of Support.

## Where is my order (WISMO)
Check the tracking in Shopify first. If a standard order has not moved for 3 business days, open a carrier trace with UPS and offer the customer a free upgrade to express on their next order.
