# Spending and Approval Policy

## Approval limits
Managers can approve purchases up to 2,500 dollars. Directors can approve up to 10,000 dollars. Anything above 10,000 dollars needs the CFO's approval, and above 50,000 dollars also the CEO's.

## Invoices
Supplier invoices are paid on net 30 terms. Invoices must reference a purchase order from NetSuite.

## Month-end close
The books are closed on the 4th business day of each month. Department heads receive their budget versus actual report on the 6th business day.
