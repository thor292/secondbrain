# Lumen Home Company Overview

## Who we are
Lumen Home is an online home decor and furniture brand founded in 2019 and based in Portland, Oregon. We sell lighting, textiles, furniture and decor directly to customers through our Shopify store, with about 120 employees.

## Mission and values
Our mission is to make well designed, long lasting home goods affordable. Our values are: customers first, own the outcome, keep it simple, and leave things better than we found them.

## Goals for FY2026
Reach 61 million dollars in revenue, keep the return rate under 9 percent, lift repeat customer rate to 38 percent, and reach a customer satisfaction score (CSAT) of 92 percent.

## Tools we use
Slack for chat, Notion for documents, Shopify for the store, Zendesk for customer support, NetSuite for finance and Ramp for expenses.
