# Shipping Policy

## Free shipping
Standard shipping is free on orders over 75 dollars within the contiguous United States. Below 75 dollars standard shipping costs 7.95 dollars.

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping costs 15 dollars and arrives in 2 business days. Furniture delivered by Hearth Freight takes 2 to 3 weeks and includes room of choice placement.

## International
We ship to Canada and the United Kingdom. International orders take 7 to 10 business days and duties are charged at checkout.
