# Brand Voice Guide

## How we sound
Warm, confident and simple. We write like a friend with good taste, not like a catalog. Short sentences, no jargon, and never more than one exclamation mark per email.

## Words we use
Home, light, crafted, everyday, cozy. We say "pieces" instead of "products" and "your space" instead of "your property".

## Words we avoid
Cheap, luxury, must-have, limited time only (unless it is true), and any claims about sustainability that legal has not approved.
