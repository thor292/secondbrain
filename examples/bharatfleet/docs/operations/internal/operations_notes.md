# BharatFleet Operations Notes

## Hubs
BharatFleet runs large-item delivery hubs in Chakan (Pune), Bhiwandi (Mumbai), Hoskote (Bengaluru) and Gurugram for about 60 client brands, mostly furniture and appliances. The fleet has 320 trucks and 140 two-person delivery crews.

## Cutoff
Client pickups booked before 11am IST are collected the same day. Pickups booked later are collected the next day.

## Delivery slots
Customers are called the day before delivery to fix a 3 hour slot. Two failed slots send the consignment back to the hub and the client is informed.

## Festive peak
From 1 October to 15 November volumes roughly double; hubs run night shifts and extra trucks are hired on 30-day contracts.
