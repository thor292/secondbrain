# BharatFleet Logistics Handbook

## Earned leave
BharatFleet employees get 15 days of paid earned leave per financial year (April to March), with no carry forward.

## Holidays
BharatFleet observes 10 paid holidays a year, including Diwali, Holi and Ganesh Chaturthi. Drivers and hub staff who work on a holiday get double pay for that day.

## Remote work
Office staff in Pune can work remotely one day per week. Drivers, delivery crews and hub staff are on site.

## Festive peak
No planned leave for hub staff and delivery crews from 1 October to 15 November.
